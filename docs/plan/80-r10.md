# R10 — Learn (v0.16.0)

The release that adds learning between tests. Teachers get Vocabulary (word lists, words with
pronunciation, flashcard assignments to classes) and Courses (units, lessons of five kinds,
release rules, progress). Students get Learn, the Course and Lesson pages, a Flashcards session
with spaced repetition, and the Practice card on Home. Word lists and courses join R7's sharing,
and a course share carries read and assign access to what the course uses.

R10 runs after R7 (`77-r7.md`: `content_shares`, the share dialog, Shared with me, make-a-copy,
the email channel and its scheduler) and R9 (`79-r9.md`), whose read models it narrows.
Vocabulary lands before Courses inside the release, because a flashcards lesson points at a word
list (70 §2 rule 7). Screens are compared with `docs/design/deck/Quizzivy Teacher.dc.html`
(Courses, course detail, Vocabulary, word list) and `Quizzivy Student.dc.html` (`learn`,
`course`, `lesson`, `flash`, and Home's Practice card). Branch `work/redesign-r10` is cut from
`develop`.

The work touches three high-risk areas named in AGENTS: `media` (a new kind and new references),
`attempts` (test lessons start through the lesson gate) and `assignments` (test lessons are
assignments). Every task that touches them runs the module's unit tests and the five canaries on
its branch point and on its last commit:

- `server/internal/modules/tests/application/tests/publish_snapshot_test.go`
- `web/tests/units/media/audio-player.test.tsx`
- `server/internal/modules/attempts/application/tests/events_test.go`
- `web/tests/units/api/client.refresh.test.ts`
- `web/tests/integration/router-chunks.test.ts`

**Deliverable:** word lists, words and pronunciation; flashcard assignments with a new-words
allowance and a daily reminder; an SM-2 variant scheduler with streaks; video as a media kind;
courses with units, reading, video, listening, flashcards and test lessons, release rules, pace
and progress; test lessons delivered as assignments; sharing and transitive access for both
kinds; the student's Learn, Course, Lesson and Flashcards pages and the Home Practice card; and
learner payloads that carry no answer key.

**Decisions taken in this file, worth a second look.**

- **The scheduler is an SM-2 variant whose first intervals are the deck's labels.** A card's state
  is ease (starts 2.5, clamped to 1.3–3.0), interval in days, repetitions and lapses. On a card
  never reviewed, or on the review after an Again: Again is due in 1 minute, Hard in 1 day, Good in
  3 days and Easy in 7 days. After that, with interval I and ease E: Hard gives `max(1,
  round(I × 1.2))`, Good `max(3, round(I × E))` and Easy `max(7, round(I × E × 1.3))`, all capped
  at 365. Ease changes by −0.20 (Again), −0.15 (Hard), 0 (Good) and +0.15 (Easy). Again counts a
  lapse and the card is re-shown later in the same session. There is no random fuzz: a
  deterministic schedule can be tested and explained to a student.
- **The grade buttons show the card's actual next interval.** For a new card they read exactly as
  the deck draws them ("Again < 1 min", "Hard 1 day", "Good 3 days", "Easy 1 week"). For a
  reviewed card they show what the button will do ("Good 8 days"), because a static label would
  be wrong from the second review on.
- **A word is "known" when its interval is at least seven days and its last grade was not
  Again.** One definition serves the student's "15 of 28 known", the teacher's "Students know it"
  and "Mastered on average". Seven days means the student answered Easy once or Good twice. The
  hardest word is the one with the highest lapse rate (lapses over repetitions, summed across
  students), among words with at least three repetitions.
- **New words arrive at the class's daily allowance, in list order.** A student in two classes
  with the same list gets the larger allowance, not the sum. A flashcards lesson ignores the
  allowance for its own list, because a lesson asks the student to meet every word in it. Due
  reviews are never capped.
- **Streaks count local days.** A day counts when the student grades at least one card between
  00:00 and 24:00 in their time zone (R4's profile setting). The streak is the run of such days
  ending today or yesterday. `practice_days` holds one row per student and day.
- **Pronunciation is an uploaded audio file per word, falling back to the browser's speech
  synthesis** (DG-95). No server-side text-to-speech: it would be a new third-party dependency
  sending word lists off-site for a feature the browser already has. The Listen button is hidden
  when neither is available.
- **Video is a new media kind, uploaded like audio and never transcoded** (DG-94). MP4 only, up to
  95 MiB and 30 minutes, sniffed from the `ftyp` box, with the duration read by the existing MP4
  probe. The size cap comes from the edge: `api.quizzivy.com` is proxied by Cloudflare, whose
  Free plan refuses a request body over 100 MB (`docs/setup/dns.md`), so a larger cap would
  answer 413 before the API saw the file. 95 MiB holds about 13 minutes at 1 Mbit/s (a 720p
  screen recording) and about 6.5 minutes at 2 Mbit/s, so for most files the size binds before
  the 30 minutes. It goes through the existing streaming upload route with its read and write
  deadlines raised for the upload (the server's 120 s `WriteTimeout` would otherwise cut it off),
  one video upload at a time per API process, so two teachers cannot fill the machine's disk.
  Presigned direct upload stays out of v1 (`15-phase-5.md`); anything larger is O-R10.1. WebM is
  refused: MP4 plays in every browser students use, and a second container would need a second
  probe. It plays in `<video controls preload="metadata">` from a signed URL; the CSP's
  `media-src` already allows R2.
- **Lesson bodies are the existing content model, edited with the existing editor, saved
  explicitly** (DG-94). Reading, video notes and listening notes are `semantic_v1` documents
  (`server/internal/shared/content`), edited with `ContentEditor` and rendered with
  `ContentView`. The lesson editor saves with a button and a dirty guard rather than autosave. A
  lesson has no publish snapshot to protect, and explicit save avoids the two flushes AGENTS
  requires of every debounced autosave.
- **A course's test and flashcards lessons may point only at the course owner's tests and word
  lists.** The command checks it under the course row lock. This keeps the whole course
  manageable by its owner, makes transitive access through a course share well defined, and lets
  R5's ownership transfer move a teacher's courses, tests and word lists together. Media is
  exempt: assets are immutable and may be shared by reference, as R7's copies already are, but a
  word or lesson may bind only an asset the actor can read (T-R7.7's readable-asset definition).
- **A test lesson is delivered as an ordinary assignment, created through the assignments
  `Create` port.** There is one assignment per test lesson per assigning teacher, targeting that
  teacher's classes that have the course, marked by `assignments.course_lesson_id`. A partial
  unique index on `(course_lesson_id, created_by)` makes the sync idempotent, and the sync takes a
  per-course advisory lock (the registry in `20-data-model.md`). Attempts, autosave, integrity,
  grading and the leak guarantees are reused unchanged. Timing, attempts, integrity and review
  rules come from the assigning teacher's assignment defaults (R4). The window opens at
  assignment and closes 365 days later. R7's in-API notification scheduler (T-R7.14) gains an
  `extend-course-windows` rule that extends a window by another 365 days when its course is still
  assigned to that teacher's classes and fewer than 30 days remain, audited as System, under the
  scheduler's advisory lock `(73819, 42)`. Its next due time joins the scheduler's candidates, so
  it adds no wake of its own. It is not a `cmd/maintenance` step: R1's window commands move
  deadlines only when an operator runs them, and R5's nightly sweep is D16's retention job, which
  applies only once `RETENTION_SWEEP_ENABLED` is set, so an extension there could stop silently.
  Release rules are enforced by the lesson gate, not by the window.
- **The lesson gate runs in the attempt start path, through a port.** `StartOrResume` asks the
  courses module whether the lesson is open for the student, only when the assignment has a
  `course_lesson_id`, and answers `409 LESSON_LOCKED` otherwise. Resuming an attempt is never
  refused, following R1's maintenance rule.
- **Course-lesson assignments stay out of places that describe the class's test schedule**:
  Home's Coming up, due-time notifications, R7's due-soon, closing and `after_close`
  result-release emails, R8's teacher calendar and the student's week, the dashboard's Today,
  "Closing in 24 h", taking now and submissions, the teacher summary's live assignments,
  Gradebook, Reports and student Grades (O-R9.2). They stay in Grading and in the teacher's
  Assignments list, labelled "{course} · lesson {n}", because someone has to grade them. The
  Settings tab shows "Managed by the course" on Test & timing and Window.
- **Release rules are evaluated per student when read, never stored and never timed.** A unit is
  open when its rule holds (open now; every published lesson of the previous unit done; or its
  date reached), when the course has started for the student's class, and, with the "One unit a
  week" pace, when `starts_on + (n − 1) weeks` has passed. Inside an open unit, lessons open in
  order, one after the previous is done. No timer flips a flag, so nothing wakes Neon.
- **Test-lesson completion is derived; other completions are stored.** Reading, video and
  listening lessons complete on "Mark as done", and a flashcards lesson when every word of its
  list has been reviewed once. These write `lesson_progress`. A test lesson is complete when the
  student has handed in an attempt on its assignment. Deriving that from `attempts` keeps
  `submit.go`, a high-risk path, free of a courses dependency.
- **A course share carries read and assign access to what the course uses, never edit access.**
  With a Can use or Can edit grant on a course, a teacher can read and assign the course, preview
  its lessons, see the published versions of its lesson tests, and read its word lists and media.
  Editing a lesson test or a word list still needs a grant on that test or list. Revoking the
  course grant does not change classes that already have the course: students keep learning in
  it, and the teacher who assigned it can still unassign their own class.
- **Copying a course copies what it points at.** R7's make-a-copy for a course copies the course,
  its units and lessons, every word list its flashcards lessons use, and every test its test
  lessons use (as new draft tests through R7's test copy, which copies bank questions on write).
  Media is referenced by id. A copied test lesson stays a draft until its copied test is
  published. The invariant above rules out a copy that points at someone else's tests.
- **"Reset progress" resets the actor's students only.** A word list shared with another teacher
  may be practised in their classes too. Resetting deletes the review state of the chosen words
  for students in the actor's classes that have the list assigned, and nobody else's.
- **"Import words" on the Vocabulary page creates a new list named after the file.** The deck's
  dialog has a file and a header toggle and no target list. The teacher renames the list
  afterwards.
- **Deleting follows what students lose.** A word is deleted outright, with its review state, as
  the deck's confirm says ("Students lose their progress on them"). A lesson is deleted outright:
  its stored completions go with it, and its test lesson's assignments are closed early and
  detached (`course_lesson_id` set NULL), so attempts and grades survive as a closed assignment.
  Word lists and courses are soft-deleted, and refused with `409` while a class has them or a
  lesson uses them.
- **Deleting a class removes its flashcard and course assignments, not students' progress.**
  `flashcard_assignments.class_id` and `course_classes.class_id` cascade: they are the class's
  configuration, and without the cascade R2's 409 on class delete would refuse every class that
  ever practised.
- **Learner payloads are their own Go types.** `StudentLesson`, `StudentCourse` and
  `FlashCard` share nothing with the teacher types, so a new teacher field cannot reach a student
  by accident. A listening lesson has no script field; its text is `body`, which is never named
  `transcript` (E2E 9's forbidden key).
- **Stats that describe named students need `people.students.read`.** The words list, the
  vocabulary cards and the course cards carry `knownPercent`, `masteredPercent` or
  `averageCompletion` only when the caller holds that key, and null otherwise, as R4's summary
  does. The stats and progress operations declare it outright.
- **The daily practice reminder needs both switches, and cards due.** It is sent when the teacher
  kept "Daily reminder at 19:00" on for the class, the student kept "Daily practice reminder" on,
  cards are due and the student has not practised today. The in-app item materialises on read,
  like R4's due-time items. The email goes out from R7's scheduler once a day at 19:00 in
  `APP_TIMEZONE`: one wake a day, not one per student.

## Open items

- **DG-94.** The lesson editor, the Publish control on a draft course, unit rename and delete,
  and the student render of video, listening, flashcards and test lessons are extrapolated from
  deck parts and need the design team's sign-off.
- **DG-95.** The per-word pronunciation upload in the word dialog is extrapolated; the speech
  synthesis fallback is interim.
- **DG-90.** Share entry points for word lists and courses follow the test's share dialog until
  the deck draws them.
- **O-R10.1 — Video limits (Thuong).** 95 MiB, 30 minutes, MP4 only, and one upload at a time are
  this file's defaults. The 95 MiB comes from Cloudflare's 100 MB request-body limit on the Free
  plan in front of `api.quizzivy.com` (`docs/setup/dns.md`); it holds about 13 minutes of 720p at
  1 Mbit/s. Longer recorded lessons need one of: presigned direct upload to R2 (outside v1,
  `15-phase-5.md`), an upload host that Cloudflare does not proxy, or a Cloudflare plan with a
  larger body limit. Each changes the edge, the cost or the security posture, so Thuong chooses.
  Default: 95 MiB through the proxied route, revisited with real usage.
- **O-R10.2 — Course test lessons in the Gradebook.** They are kept out of Insights (O-R9.2).
  Default: out.
- **O-R10.3 — Listening scripts.** A listening lesson has notes but no separate script, so no
  field collides with E2E 9's `transcript` key. If teachers want a revealable script, it needs a
  name, a review rule and a leak-test change. Default: none; the teacher writes it in the notes.
- **O-R10.4 — Levels as ranges.** The deck's fixtures show "B1–B2" on course cards; lists and
  courses store one level, as the dialogs draw. Default: one level.
- **DG-83.** The empty, loading and error states of every new screen ship from deck primitives
  and are listed for sign-off.
