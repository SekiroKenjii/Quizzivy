# R8 — Schedule (v0.14.0)

The class timetable the deck draws and the product lacks: weekly class sessions with per-date
changes, the teacher's Calendar with its New session dialog, Attendance with a "this term" rate,
terms managed by admins, and the student's "This week". A class's schedule line stops being free
text once the class has sessions; it is derived from them, and the free text remains the fallback.
The public join preview gains the schedule and the room (D5), never a count (DG-02).

Screens are compared with:

- `docs/design/deck/Quizzivy Teacher.dc.html`: `r.calendar`, the New session form, and
  `r.attendance`
- `Quizzivy Student.dc.html`: `schedule` (This week), the Classes page and its Join dialog
- `Quizzivy Sign in.dc.html`: `#join`'s preview card

Where the deck is silent, `docs/design/gaps.md` decides. DG-92 settles weekly series and the
attendance statuses. DG-93 puts terms in Admin settings. DG-81 names `/app/week`, and DG-83 the
empty states.

R8 needs:

- R4: the class `schedule_label` and `room`, the dashboard's `today`, `useIdlePolling` and the
  console composites
- R5 (`75-r5.md`): the `organization` module (T-R5.5), Admin › System settings
  (`web/src/features/organization/`, T-R5.28a) and the Admin Classes table (T-R5.11, T-R5.25)
- R7: the weekly summary, and one contract step, T-R8.1

Ordering rule 7 puts R8 before R9, because the gradebook and reports read attendance. Branch
`work/redesign-r8` is cut from `develop`, and feature branches are `feature/t-r8-<nn>-<slug>`.

**Deliverable:**

- Weekly session series per class, with one-off sessions and per-date changes: move, change the
  room, add a note, cancel.
- The Calendar week view and phone agenda, with assignment opens and closes beside the sessions.
- Attendance per session (present, late, absent or unmarked), with each student's rate this term.
- Terms, created and edited by admins.
- The student's This week, and the timetable line on the student's class cards.
- A derived schedule and room on every class surface and on the public join preview.

- The isolation suite covers every new `/teacher/*` and `/app/*` operation, with an `x-resource`

**Decisions taken in this file, worth a second look.**

- **Sessions come from weekly series, and an occurrence has no row until it needs one** (DG-92).
  - A series row holds the class, weekday, start time, length, room, first and last date, and
    time zone.
  - Occurrences are computed for the range a screen asks for.
  - `class_sessions` holds three kinds of row: one-off sessions, per-date changes to an
    occurrence, and occurrences that have attendance.
  - Reads never write. Editing a series changes every future occurrence without rewriting stored
    rows, and past attendance stays attached to its date.
  - Materialising on read was rejected. It writes on GET, and it leaves stale rows behind every
    series edit.
- **An occurrence's id is stable whether or not a row exists.** A series occurrence is
  `{seriesId}.{yyyymmdd}`, and a one-off session is its uuid. The contract treats both as an
  opaque `sessionId`. A write to an occurrence without a row creates it first, in the same
  transaction.
- **Editing a repeating session applies from a date on and never rewrites the past.** The series
  is split at that date. If today's occurrence already has attendance, the change starts
  tomorrow. Per-date changes after the split date that describe the old pattern are dropped
  unless they carry attendance, and the dialog says how many.
- **"Repeat every week" runs to the end of the term containing the first date.** With no term,
  it is open-ended until the teacher stops it. An open-ended series is bounded at read time by
  the requested range, and no series may be scheduled more than two years out.
- **The statuses are present, late and absent, plus unmarked, which is the absence of a row**
  (DG-92). Excused is not built. Late counts as attended in the rate. The rate is
  `(present + late) ÷ (present + late + absent)` over the class's non-cancelled sessions in the
  current term, meaning the term containing today in the teacher's zone. Without a term it covers
  every session and reads "overall". Unmarked sessions are left out, so a teacher who skips a day
  does not lower anyone's rate.
- **Attendance is taken only for a session that has started,** allowing 15 minutes of grace,
  and never for a cancelled one. Marks for a date that has not happened would put
  fiction into R9's Attendance KPI.
- **Terms are organisation-wide, never overlap, and belong to Admin settings** (DG-93, 70 §4.1:
  `system.settings.write`). An `EXCLUDE USING gist` over `daterange(starts_on, ends_on, '[]')`
  enforces the overlap rule in the database. It needs no extension, because range types carry
  their own GiST operator class.
- **The schedule line is formatted by the web from structured slots.** The server returns
  `{slots: [{weekday, startTime, durationMinutes}], label}`, where `label` is the class's free
  text, and the web formats the slots per locale: "T2 · T4 · T6, 18:00" and "Mon · Wed · Fri,
  18:00".
  - A server-built string would be in one language.
  - The room is the class's room, else the single room all active series share, else none.
- **The join preview adds the weekly pattern and the room, nothing else.** No session dates,
  notes, cancellations, member counts or session counts. The preview is what a teacher prints on
  a flyer, reachable only with a valid code, and it keeps R2's rate budget.
- **Sessions cascade with their class, and attendance restricts it.** A class that has attendance
  is never hard-deleted, just as one with attempts is not. The delete answers 409 with
  `referencedBy: attendance`, a value T-R8.2 adds to T-R2.13's constraint-name map. A class with
  sessions but no attendance deletes cleanly.
