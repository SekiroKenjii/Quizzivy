# Design gaps — requests to the design team

The deck in `docs/design/deck/` is the single source of truth for the UI. This register lists
what the deck does not draw, what it draws in two incompatible ways, and where a decision
overrides it: DG-01 to DG-11 were taken with Thuong on 2026-09-26, DG-12 to DG-19 on 2026-10-03
with the second import, under his delegation; DG-117 on 2026-10-04, likewise. Each entry says what
we build until the design team answers ("Meanwhile") and which release needs the answer
(`docs/plan/70-redesign-overview.md`).

DG-108 to DG-116 were opened on 2026-10-04 with the third import, which redrew the Test builder's
editor pane and the import review's "Question" field. Their "Meanwhile" is the default the plan
builds (`docs/plan/74-r4.md`), not a decision: each stays Open until Thuong or the design team
answers. That import fully answers no earlier entry; DG-35, DG-36 and DG-60 are partly answered
and say so, and DG-63, DG-69, DG-80, DG-83 and DG-90 carry a note of what it added.

DG-119 was opened on 2026-10-04 with R3's documents (`docs/plan/73-r3.md`, T-R3.14). Its
"Meanwhile" is what R3 built. DG-80 holds the engine's frames for sign-off as one list.

When a new export answers an entry, mark it **Answered** with the export date, and delete the
"Meanwhile" rule once the product matches the drawing.

Status: **Open** — needs the design team. **Decided** — Thuong decided; the deck should be
redrawn to match. **Answered** — resolved by a later export.
