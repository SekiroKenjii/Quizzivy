what the deck does not draw, what it draws in two incompatible ways, and where a decision
overrides it: DG-01 to DG-11 were taken with Thuong on 2026-09-26, DG-12 to DG-19 on 2026-10-03
with the second import, under his delegation; DG-117 on 2026-10-04, likewise. Each entry says what
we build until the design team answers ("Meanwhile") and which release needs the answer
(`docs/plan/70-redesign-overview.md`).
