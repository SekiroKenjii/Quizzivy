Grading when nothing is left to mark

These are the two states the deck never drew: an empty queue, and papers that are marked but not finished. Below are the three answers you asked for, both states at 1280, 768 and 360 in light and dark, the other cases, and every English line. The rules stay as they are: Finish is always explicit and nothing finishes automatically. Nothing here needs an API change.

Source: work/deck-d4 · GradingPage.tsx, gradingMessages.ts DG-136 filter chip
Finish from the row? No. The row's button is Review, and Finish is one step away.
  • The row comes from the attempt list, which has no score and no answered count, and nothing in it says a paper is blank. A Finish button on the row would send a result the teacher hasn't seen, including a 0 for a blank paper or a result on a flagged one.
  • Review opens a check inside the row. It's built from a fresh read of the paper and shows the score, how many answers were given, how the paper was marked and any flag. Finish grading sits under it.
  • After a Finish, the next paper's check opens with focus on its Finish button. For 14 auto-marked quizzes that's one Review, then 14 Finishes, each one after seeing the score.
Beside the queue too? No. It stays below the panes, folded to one line while answers are waiting.
  • While answers are waiting, the job is marking them. The answer card already offers Finish once a paper's last hand-marked answer has a score.
  • The queue column is 300px wide and already scrolls. A second list there would squeeze both, and at 768 there's no room for it.
  • The header line links to it: "18 answers left from 6 students · 26 papers ready to finish". The link unfolds the card and moves focus to it.
A long list? Group it by assignment, five papers per group, then "Show N more".
  • The page already reads the list to the end, so paging would only hide papers that are already loaded.
  • Teachers finish an assignment's papers together, and a test is usually all auto-marked or partly hand-marked, so the group is the unit they work in.
  • Oldest hand-in first, for groups and for rows. The 180px scroll box goes, and the card grows with the page. A single group (a narrowed queue) drops its header.

The page at 1280, 768 and 360

The three frames share one state. Use the bar above to switch states. Inside the frames, Review opens a check, Finish grading removes the paper and opens the next one, and Show more, Refresh and the filter chip all work. The shell is drawn plainly so the page stands out: 248px sidebar from 768, a drawer below that, and 28 or 14px gutters, as the page-width proposal sets them.

  1. 1Header line. An empty queue reads "No answers left." instead of "0 answers left from 0 students.". While answers are waiting, the line adds a link, "26 papers ready to finish", that unfolds the card and moves focus to it. The DG-136 chip comes last, and removing it moves focus to the heading, as built.
  2. 2Empty state. A dashed card with a 44px tile, a 16/600 title, one 14px sentence (46ch at most) and at most one action. Its variant comes from the queue and the check (see the table). Variant (b)'s action, "Review 26 papers", opens the oldest paper's check and moves focus to its Finish.
  3. 3Card title and sentence. "Ready to finish", then a count of the papers that pass the filter. The sentence says what Finish does for the student and why a score may not show yet.
  4. 4Refresh. This replaces "Rescan". It sits beside "Checked 10:42", the time of the last complete check, taken from the query's last update. While it runs, the button reads "Checking…" and the rows stay where they are. It still refetches both the list and the queue. At 360 it's a 44px icon button.
  5. 5Group header. The test title and the group's paper count, on a --sidebar strip. Rows are grouped by assignmentId. If two groups share a title, the header adds the group's earliest hand-in date.
  1. 6Row. Avatar, name, "Handed in Mon 22 Sep, 21:14", plus "Time ran out" for timed_out and "Flagged" for flagged. All of this comes from the attempt list row. The one button, "Review" or "Close", opens and closes the check and carries aria-expanded. Only one check is open at a time.
  2. 7Check. It's today's candidate panel, moved into the row. It reads getAttemptForReview and shows the score (earned / total), the answers given (non-null answers), and how the paper was marked:
    • "You marked n answers" when n answers need hand marking and all have a manual score;
    • "Marked automatically" when no answer needs hand marking;
    • "Left blank" when no answer was given.
    A flagged paper gets a warning line, and a blank one a line about the 0. Finish stays disabled unless pendingManual === 0, as built.
  3. After Finish. The row leaves the list, a toast says "Grading finished for Trần Minh Anh.", and the next paper in order opens its check. Focus goes to that check's Finish once it loads. A polite live region says "Grading finished for Trần Minh Anh. Next: Lê Hoàng Nam.". When it was the last paper, focus goes to the empty state's title.
  4. 8Folded while answers are waiting. The card sits below the panes. Folded, it shows only the title, the count and Show. It's drawn when there is at least one paper to finish, or when the check failed.
  5. 9Show more. Five rows per group, then "Show 9 more" or "Show fewer". A group opens fully if the next check falls past its fifth row.

Which state shows

"All graded" shows only after a complete check of the papers. While the check is running, or after it fails, the page says only what it knows: no answers are left to mark.
