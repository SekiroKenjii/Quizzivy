Generate for standalone questions, a section and a whole test

The answer to the brief's three parts. A refines the hotfix's style control and section toggle, B draws the whole-test plan, and C puts the credit ceiling next to Generate. Everything is built from the panel as it is on main: the 560px Sheet, the panel's fields, the item card, the run steps and the credit note. Only three pieces are new: a radio card, a section picker and a plan row. Nothing here changes the H1 contract. The mockups are in Vietnamese and switch to English from the bar.

Source: main · AiPanel.tsx, kinds/generate.ts, AiPanelField.tsx, AiItemCard.tsx CR-001 · H1 v0.11.10 · H2 QC-739-1 to 3 · DG-211
A.1 · Cards or segmented? Two cards with one line each. The hotfix's segmented control can ship first, with the same values.
  • The choice changes three things: what the box asks for, whether the passage chips show, and where the questions go. Two words can't explain that, but one line under each can.
  • Teachers pick it once per session, so 40px more height costs little. The cards sit side by side in the drawer and stack at 360. They're one radio group: one tab stop, and arrow keys move between them.
  • Pick the default from the open section, not from the page. Under the CR's rule, opening Generate from Part 3 (grammar, no groups) lands on Theo bài đọc with Part 1's passage chosen. That's one line in initial.
A.3 · Showing and changing the section A "Thêm vào phần" card shows the section and its count, with the fit toggle under it.
  • The card names the section and says what AI will find there: "6 câu · có hướng dẫn". Đổi lists every saved section, including empty ones (QC-739-2), and marks the open one "đang mở".
  • The toggle sits inside the card because it's about that section. Its label shortens to "Hợp với phần này", since the title is already right above it.
  • When the toggle is off, the card stays and still says where the questions will go. We suggest pinning the destination at Run in both cases. The CR pins it only when the section is read.
B · One section or the whole test A segmented control at the top of the body, shown only in the builder.
  • Tabs in the header would read as two tools. It's one panel: one header, one footer, one credit line and the same result cards.
  • Each scope keeps its own values while you switch. The control hides once a run starts, and Làm lại từ đầu brings it back.
  • The bank and Explain results never show it, because they have no sections.
B.1 · A plan table in a 560px drawer Rows that open: each row shows the section and its count, and the other options appear once the count isn't 0.
  • Four columns don't fit in 524px of content with Vietnamese labels. Every row starts at 0, so most rows stay on one line. Types and Theo từng bài đọc / Câu độc lập appear under a row once it gets a count.
  • From 768 the rows sit under a header ("Phần · Số câu mới"). Below 768 they become cards and the count control fills the width. The Sheet needs no new width.
  • In a passage section the count is per passage, because each passage is one batch: "2 bài đọc × 3 câu = 6 câu, mỗi bài vào nhóm của nó".
B.2 and C · Limits and cost Limits stay quiet until crossed. The credit ceiling goes where "N left" is today.
  • The total line is the brief's "Tổng 16 câu · 3 phần". When a plan goes over 40 questions or 5 sections and passages, a warning says which limit and how to fix it, and Generate turns off.
  • "Tối đa 800 tín dụng · còn 1.840 trong tháng này" replaces "Còn 1.840 tín dụng". We wrote "tối đa", not "tối đa khoảng", because the figure is the reservation and a real ceiling. After the run, the footer shows what was used, as it does today.
  • When fewer credits are left than the ceiling, the line becomes a warning and Generate turns off, because the run would be refused anyway.
B.6 · 30 to 45 seconds A status for each section, if the server can report it.
  • Each section shows Đang viết, then its count, or Chưa tạo được. A bar and "2/3 phần xong" sit above the rows. One line says closing the panel keeps the result, which has been true since v0.11.8.
  • The CR's operation is one request with one answer. To show real status for each section, the server must report it by streaming or polling. Without that, every row stays Đang viết with the time so far. Please don't show a fake 2/3.
