Foundations

Everything on the other sheets is assembled from this page. The tokens are copied verbatim from web/src/index.css; the components are the shadcn/ui new-york primitives constrained by spec §12. If a screen needs something that is not here, that is a signal to add it here first — not to invent a one-off.

1 · Colour

Zinc surfaces, charcoal primary, semantic colour only where it carries meaning. Nothing decorative gets a hue. The token names below are the only way a component may reference a colour — that is what makes the dark theme (P1) a token swap rather than a rewrite. Try the Tối toggle above: no rule in the kit changes.

Forbidden by §12

Decorative gradients · glassmorphism / backdrop blur · pulsing rings · glow · radii above rounded-lg · emoji in UI chrome · brand colour on a non-semantic surface. A green badge means the answer was right, never “this row is nice”.

4 · Motion, focus, and the rules that survive review

Motion

150ms ease-out on state change. No entrance animation — a list that fades in is a list that arrives late. Dialogs appear; they do not spring. prefers-reduced-motion disables the rest.

The one exception worth having: the timer’s minute change is not animated at all, because a moving clock pulls the eye away from the question.

Focus & keyboard

A 2px neutral ring, offset 2px, on every interactive element. Never a coloured glow. Tab order follows reading order.

⌘K command palette A–E chọn đáp án ←→ chuyển câu F đánh dấu

Touch & reach

Student targets are ≥44px and live in the bottom third of the phone screen. Admin targets may be 28–32px because the teacher has a mouse and a 768px+ viewport (§1.1).

Nothing destructive ever sits next to a frequently-tapped control. “Nộp bài” and “Thoát” are never adjacent.

F-11 Side columns — two roles, one width each

Earlier drafts of this deck drew the right-hand panel at three widths (G-01 and A-04 at 20rem, G-07 at 24rem, S-08 at 18rem) because each board was drawn for its own screen. Four widths for one component is drift, not design: it gives the next screen no answer to “how wide is this?”. There are two roles here, and each gets one width. The boards above have been redrawn to match.

Panel · right · 20rem

w-80 border-l p-5 space-y-5 — details what is selected in the middle, or summarises what the middle is building. G-01 summary · G-07 student · A-04 question settings · S-08 navigator.

Rail · left · 14rem

w-56 border-r p-4 space-y-5 — filters what the middle shows. Narrower on purpose: it holds checkboxes and chips, and at panel width it would be mostly empty. A-06 bank filters.

Both run the full height of the content area beside the scroll, never inside it, and scroll by themselves when their own content is taller than the screen — a filter rail that scrolls away with the results is a rail you cannot reach while reading them. Neither is sticky; sticky is what you reach for when the column is in the wrong place.
