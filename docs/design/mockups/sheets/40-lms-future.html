LMS vision

§1.2’s fourth goal: “the next feature is a new folder under features/, not a rewrite”. This sheet is what that sentence looks like as pictures. Nothing here is v1 scope — the value of drawing it now is that it names the three or four decisions v1 must not accidentally close, and the two it should deliberately close in its favour.

1 · One structural idea does all the work

Everything an LMS adds — vocabulary sets, homework, pronunciation drills, readings, videos — is a thing a student is asked to do, that produces a record, that may carry a score. v1 already has that shape; it just calls it a test. Widening the noun from test to activity is the whole migration, and it costs almost nothing while there is exactly one kind.

What v1 should do now — and what it should not

Cheap now, expensive later

  • Keep the frontend route and folder names generic where they already are — features/assignments, features/take-test. The second is the only one that names a type; a later rename to features/activity-player is a folder move, not a redesign.
  • Keep role as an enum with room. §1.1 already asks for this so a limited teacher can appear later; the guards must never test role === 'admin' where they mean “may grade”.
  • Keep the class as the unit of assignment. Courses attach to classes later; a design that assigns to individuals only would have to be undone.
  • Keep media immutable and referenced, never copied (§11.1). A vocabulary set reusing a listening clip depends on exactly that property.

Do not build now

  • An activity type column with one value. A polymorphic table with a single member is speculative complexity; the rename is a migration when there are two, and it is a cheap one.
  • A course hierarchy nobody fills in. Twelve tests do not need modules. The teacher will tell you when they do, by asking for it.
  • A plugin architecture for question types. Five types, one switch statement, and a folder per type is faster to read and faster to change than a registry.
  • Multi-tenant scoping (§1.3). One practice, one teacher. Adding an org id “just in case” costs every query for years.
L-02 Navigation over three releases

The sidebar is the product’s table of contents, and rearranging it is the change users feel most. Growing it by adding groups rather than reordering items keeps the muscle memory intact — which is why the group headings exist in v1 while they are arguably still unnecessary.

“Đề thi” and “Ngân hàng câu hỏi” merge into “Thư viện nội dung” at v3, once there are six content types and a separate nav item for each would be absurd. That is the one planned disruption — worth flagging early so it is a decision rather than an accident.
Every new item lands inside an existing group until v3 adds two groups at the bottom. Nothing above ever moves down. A teacher who learned “Học viên is the seventh item” stays right for two releases.
Above roughly twelve items the palette carries the load. That is the threshold where the sidebar stops being a menu and becomes a shortcut bar for the five destinations someone actually uses.

Locked items are shown, not hidden. “Mở 01/09” at 60% opacity tells the student the week has five things in it. Hiding future work makes a course feel like a series of surprises.
The vocabulary drill reuses the option row verbatim. Same component, same key hints, same 44px target. A student who has taken one test already knows how to answer.
“Không nhớ từ này” is a first-class button. Spaced repetition needs an honest signal, and a student who guesses to avoid admitting they forgot poisons their own schedule.
No streak flames, no confetti, no XP. §12 forbids the decoration, and a private practice with one teacher does not need a slot machine — “Chuỗi đúng: 6” is the honest version of the same information.
L-05 Gradebook v3 · /admin/gradebook

Students down, activities across. Reading a row tells you about a person; reading a column tells you about your teaching. The cell is a number and nothing else — no heat map, because a wall of colour is exactly the “rich analytics dashboard” §1.3 rules out, and it makes the one number that matters harder to find.

The class-average row is the point of the screen. One weak column across every student is a question about the test; one weak row across every column is a question about a student. Making both readable in one glance is the whole design.
Red appears exactly once, on a score far below the rest. If every below-average cell were tinted, nothing would stand out and the teacher would learn to ignore the colour.
“chưa nộp” and “—” are different facts. One is a missing piece of work; the other is an activity that was never assigned to that student. Collapsing them into a zero is how a gradebook lies.

v1.1 Chấm theo câu hỏi · mẫu nhận xét Không cần dữ liệu mới, tiết kiệm nhiều giờ nhất trên mỗi giờ code. Làm ngay khi lớp đầu tiên chạy hết một chu kỳ chấm.
v1.1 Dark mode Đã là P1 trong §16 và chi phí gần bằng không nếu token giữ kỷ luật. Học viên làm bài buổi tối là ca dùng thật.
v2 Khoá học → tuần → hoạt động Cần khi số đề vượt khoảng 20 và giáo viên bắt đầu dạy theo giáo trình chứ không theo từng bài. Trước đó là cấu trúc rỗng.
v2 Bộ từ vựng · lặp ngắt quãng Loại hoạt động thứ hai — đây là lúc đổi tên test thành activity, khi đã có hai thành viên thật.
v2 Lịch · thông báo lớp Chỉ hiển thị lại dữ liệu đã có (mốc bài giao) cộng một bảng thông báo. Rẻ, và cắt hẳn kênh nhắn tin ngoài app.
v3 Sổ điểm · báo cáo kỹ năng Cần đủ nhiều hoạt động đã chấm mới có nghĩa. Phụ thuộc vào việc gắn thẻ câu hỏi từ v1 — thẻ là dữ liệu duy nhất không thể lấp ngược.
v3 Vai trò giáo viên & trợ giảng Khi có người thứ hai dạy. Guard phải hỏi “được phép làm gì” chứ không hỏi “có phải admin không” — điều này phải đúng từ v1.
