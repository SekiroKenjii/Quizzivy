Brand & error screens

The brand is Thuong's, delivered as a finished kit. This sheet does not redraw any part of it — every graphic below is a <use> of a symbol generated verbatim from docs/design/brand/svg/. What the sheet adds is the part a kit cannot carry on its own: which variant goes on which surface inside this product, and what happens at the sizes the interface actually uses.

2 · The three failure screens

Same two-panel shape as login: charcoal on the left carrying the wordmark and one quiet drawing, the answer and the actions on the right at max-w-sm. Below lg the panel drops and the card carries the wordmark itself — the same rule AuthLayout already follows, because half a phone screen of brand is half a screen the student cannot act in.

The drawings share a grammar so the three read as one family: a dotted path, and the thing at the end of it. Geometry only, one stroke weight, white at two opacities. §12 rules out alarm iconography and these screens are where that matters most — a student who hits a 403 has done nothing wrong, and a broken page is the product's fault, not theirs.

E-01 404 — không tìm thấy trang * (catch-all)

The drawing: pages that exist, and the dotted route running past the last one to a page that does not. The path itself is the point — the link was real, the destination is not, which is exactly what a 404 means and what the copy says in words.

Nền tảng luyện thi tiếng Anh dành riêng cho lớp học của bạn.

Không tìm thấy trang

Đường dẫn này không tồn tại hoặc đã bị thay đổi.

/app/assignments/8f2c-unit-5

Nếu bạn tới đây từ đường dẫn giáo viên gửi, hãy báo lại để giáo viên gửi lại link mới.

The failed path is on screen. A student reporting “nó báo lỗi” is unactionable; a student reporting /app/assignments/8f2c… tells the teacher whether the assignment was deleted or the link was mistyped.
Two actions, ranked. Home is the primary because it always works; back is secondary because it often returns to the same dead link. Both beat leaving the student on a page with nothing to press.
No “404”. The number means nothing to a fifteen-year-old and everything to us. It stays in the console and out of the copy.
E-02 Lỗi ngoài dự kiến ErrorBoundary

The drawing: a page of ordered rows where one has come apart and slipped out of line. Something inside broke — not the student's route, not their permission. The one bright element in the composition is the break, and nothing about it is red.

Đã xảy ra lỗi

Một lỗi ngoài dự kiến đã xảy ra. Bạn có thể tải lại trang để tiếp tục.

Mã lỗi a41f-9c02

Nếu tải lại vẫn lỗi, gửi mã này cho giáo viên — mã cho biết chính xác yêu cầu nào đã hỏng.

The error id keeps its own block. Below a separator, centred, with the copy button beside it — it is not an action, it is a thing to read out. It matches the API's requestId, which is what makes it worth showing at all.
Reload first, home second. Most unexpected errors are transient, so the cheapest recovery leads. Home is there for the ones that are not.
No apology, no stack trace. “Rất tiếc…” adds a line and no information; a stack trace adds fear. The footnote tells them what the code is for, which is the only thing they can act on.
E-03 Không có quyền truy cập /403 · §5.4

The drawing: the same dotted path, arriving at a boundary that is simply closed. §5.4 insists this is a page and not a redirect, precisely so the misconfiguration is visible — so the screen has to be legible about which account is being refused, which is the one fact the student needs and the one thing the current wording leaves out.

Học viên và giáo viên dùng hai khu vực khác nhau. Trang này thuộc khu vực của giáo viên.

The signed-in account is the whole screen. The commonest real cause is a student signed in with the wrong Google account, or a teacher on their personal one. Naming the address turns a dead end into a two-second fix.
“Đăng nhập bằng tài khoản khác”, not “Đăng xuất”. Sign-out states what it takes away; this states what it gets them. Same call to POST /auth/logout, better sentence.
The footnote explains the model once. Two areas, two audiences. It is the sentence that stops the second report of the same confusion.
E-04 Dưới lg — panel biến mất < 1024px
